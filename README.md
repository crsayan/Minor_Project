# Effect of Cooling Velocity on Battery Temperature

**Overview**

This project focuses on studying the thermal behavior of a lithium-ion battery pack under different cooling conditions. ANSYS simulations were used to examine the influence of airflow velocity on battery temperature distribution and heat dissipation. The study was aimed at understanding the cooling conditions required for better thermal management and energy-efficient operation.

**Features**

* Formulated a mathematical approach for analyzing battery thermal behavior.
* Conducted ANSYS simulations for airflow velocities of **2 m/s, 5 m/s, and 10 m/s**.
* Compared the effect of different airflow conditions on battery cooling.
* Examined temperature variations using **numerical and graphical analysis**.
* Identified a suitable cooling velocity based on thermal performance and cooling requirements.
* Studied the potential application of the findings in **electric vehicle and energy-storage systems**.
